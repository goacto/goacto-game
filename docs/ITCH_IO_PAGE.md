## Detailed Description

### Markdown for itch.io page:

```
# Mindscape by GOACTO

**Growing Ourselves And Contributing To Others**

You are Agent Goacto, a young alien aboard the intergalactic cruise ship "Stellar Wanderer." Your mission: help a human (you!) achieve meaningful growth through the game "Human Potential."

## What Makes Mindscape Different

This isn't a game that pretends to help you grow. **Your real-world actions ARE the game.**

- Complete a 25-minute focus session? Your mindscape evolves.
- Build a daily habit streak? New aspects of your world awaken.
- Journal your reflections? Deeper zones unlock.
- Miss a day? No punishment. Just a gentle "welcome back."

## Features

**Focus Sessions** - Pomodoro-style 25-minute deep work timer with ambient sounds (space, rain, forest, ship)

**Habit Tracking** - Build streaks with grace day recovery. Never punished, always encouraged.

**6 Aspects of Growth** - Discipline, Courage, Creativity, Compassion, Wisdom, Vitality. Each awakens as you grow.

**Living World** - Your mindscape transforms from a barren platform to a flourishing paradise based on your real progress.

**Personal Operating System** - Write scripts for your life. Organize habits as code. Debug limiting beliefs.

**Campaign Story** - 10 chapters following Agent Goacto's journey to earn their Contribution Certification.

**7 Possible Endings** - Your habits, choices, and growth style determine your unique ending.

**No Microtransactions** - Earn everything through real self-improvement. Focus coins unlock real-world discounts.

## Controls
- WASD / Arrow Keys to move
- Space to interact
- Touch controls on mobile
- Works in browser (no download needed)

## Play Now
Available as a free web game. Desktop builds coming soon.

## Built With
- Godot 4.6 (Mobile Renderer)
- 100% procedural polygon graphics (no external art assets)
- Original audio design
```
