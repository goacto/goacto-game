================================================================================
VOICE DESIGN PROMPTS (for ElevenLabs Voice Design)
================================================================================

Use these prompts in ElevenLabs Voice Design to create custom voices.
Go to: Voices > Add Voice > Voice Design > Enter prompt below

--- NARRATOR ---
A deep, resonant male narrator voice with a cinematic quality. Speaks slowly
and deliberately, like narrating an epic space documentary. Rich baritone with
gravitas and wonder. British or mid-Atlantic accent. Warm but authoritative,
like Morgan Freeman meets David Attenborough narrating a cosmic journey.

--- GOACTO (Main Character) ---
A young, gender-neutral alien voice that sounds like an enthusiastic teenager.
Warm and curious with bright, optimistic energy. Speaks with wonder and
determination. Slightly higher pitch, clear articulation. Think of an eager
young explorer discovering something amazing for the first time. American
accent, friendly and approachable.

--- LUMINA (Goacto's Mother) ---
A wise, gentle female voice with maternal warmth. Middle-aged, calm and
knowing. Speaks with a slight smile in her voice, nurturing but not
condescending. Soft and melodic with subtle playfulness. Think of a loving
mother who knows more than she lets on. Slight ethereal quality, like she
holds ancient wisdom.

--- SYSTEM (Ship Computer) ---
A clean, precise synthetic voice that still has warmth. Gender-neutral,
slightly robotic but not cold. Clinical and informative like a helpful AI
assistant. Clear enunciation, measured pace. Think of a friendly ship computer
that genuinely wants to help. Slight electronic undertone but still pleasant
to listen to.

--- DISCIPLINE (Spirit Guide) ---
A powerful, ancient male voice with deep resonance and commanding presence.
Speaks with absolute certainty and calm authority. Low pitch, measured pace,
every word has weight. Think of an ancient warrior monk or a wise king who
has seen millennia. Encouraging but firm, like a mentor who believes in you
completely. Slight echo or reverb quality.

================================================================================
GENERATION TIPS
================================================================================

1. Generate all lines for ONE character before switching voices
2. Keep the same browser tab open to maintain voice consistency
3. If a generation sounds off, regenerate (don't adjust settings mid-session)
4. Add "..." for natural pauses in speech
5. For SYSTEM voice, add slight pauses between technical terms
6. For emotional lines, use CAPS sparingly: "I could actually HELP someone?!"
7. Export as MP3, then batch convert to OGG using:

   ffmpeg -i input.mp3 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 6 output.ogg
