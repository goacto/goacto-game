# Courage Awakens - Voice Lines

Speaker: **Courage** (bold, energetic, fiery. Speaks with exclamation. Like a fearless warrior with a grin.)

> NOT TODAY, shadows! Not on my watch!

**Tone:** Explosive entrance, battle cry, triumphant.

### 2. `courage_intro.ogg`
> I am Courage! The one who runs TOWARD the scary stuff! Your human has been building strength. Time to USE it.

**Tone:** Confident, excited, rallying.

### 3. `courage_teaching.ogg`
> Fear is just excitement without breath. Doubt is just wisdom asking for evidence.

**Tone:** Wise-but-brave, reframing fear as something positive.

### 4. `courage_arena.ogg`
> We don't eliminate them - we FIGHT alongside them! The Training Arena awaits, when your human is ready.

**Tone:** Eager, ready for battle, welcoming the challenge.

> And wisdom without courage is paralysis. We need each other.

**Tone:** Playful wink, acknowledging the partnership.
