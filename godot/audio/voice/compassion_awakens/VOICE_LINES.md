# Compassion Awakens - Voice Lines

Speaker: **Compassion** (warm, gentle, nurturing. Speaks softly with deep kindness. Like a loving presence that sees your worth.)

## Files to Generate

### 1. `compassion_emerge.ogg`
> Hello, little one. You've been working so hard.

**Tone:** Tender, warm arrival. Like a hug in voice form.

### 2. `compassion_intro.ogg`
> I am Compassion. The one who reminds your human that they are enough. Even when they stumble. Especially then.

**Tone:** Firm but gentle. The "especially then" lands with weight.

### 3. `compassion_balance.ogg`
> Growth is beautiful, but so is rest. Achievement matters, but so does acceptance.

**Tone:** Balancing, reassuring, correcting the drive to overwork.

### 4. `compassion_courage.ogg`
> You push them forward. I catch them when they fall. Together, we make sure they keep going without breaking.

**Tone:** Speaking to Courage with deep affection and partnership.

### 5. `compassion_wholeness.ogg`
> Your human has grown remarkably, Goacto. But remember: the goal isn't perfection. It's wholeness.

**Tone:** Wise, loving, the central message of compassion.

### 6. `compassion_welcome.ogg`
> Now you're getting it. Welcome to the full family.

**Tone:** Warm smile, welcoming, a moment of belonging.

## Audio Specs
- Format: OGG Vorbis, 44100 Hz, Mono, Normalize to -3dB
