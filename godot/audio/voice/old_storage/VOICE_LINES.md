# Old Storage Room - Voice Lines

Speaker: **Goacto** (young, curious, wonder-filled alien teenager)

## Files to Generate

### 1. `room_enter.ogg`
> *Dust particles float in the dim light...*
> This room hasn't been opened in years. Ancient boxes and covered furniture fill the space.
> *Something glows faintly in the corner...*

**Tone:** Hushed, awed, slightly nervous. Whispering at first, then growing curious.

### 2. `console_found.ogg`
> You found Great-Elder Zyx's Mindscape Console!
> *The ancient device hums warmly in your hands, as if recognizing its new owner.*
> This is how Goactorians connect with their human companions. Take it back to your room and set it up!

**Tone:** Excited, amazed, emotional connection to ancestor. Building from surprise to determination.

### 3. `examine_chest.ogg`
> A beautifully carved chest with Goactorian symbols.
> It belonged to Great-Elder Zyx before they passed.
> *You run your fingers over the intricate patterns, feeling a connection to your ancestors.*

**Tone:** Reverent, gentle, nostalgic. Quiet respect for the elder.

### 4. `examine_shelf.ogg`
> Old data crystals and memory cubes line the shelves.
> Labels have faded beyond reading.
> *These contain knowledge from generations past, waiting to be rediscovered.*

**Tone:** Curious, slightly sad about lost knowledge, hopeful about rediscovery.

### 5. `examine_portrait.ogg`
> A holographic family portrait from three generations ago.
> You recognize Great-Elder Zyx as a young Goactorian.
> *They're holding a small device that looks familiar... is that the Mindscape Console?*

**Tone:** Surprised recognition, connecting dots, excited realization.

## Audio Specs
- Format: OGG Vorbis
- Sample rate: 44100 Hz
- Mono channel
- Normalize to -3dB
