# Creativity Awakens - Voice Lines

Speaker: **Creativity** (ecstatic, rapid-fire, playful. Speaks in bursts of wonder. Like an excited inventor who can't contain themselves.)

## Files to Generate

### 1. `creativity_emerge.ogg`
> Possible? POSSIBLE? My dear agent, "possible" is just a starting point!

**Tone:** Explosive joy, cascading energy, can barely contain excitement.

### 2. `creativity_intro.ogg`
> Guilty as charged! I am the part of your human that sees what COULD be, not just what is.

**Tone:** Playful confession, proud of being creative.

### 3. `creativity_connections.ogg`
> Every problem they solve, every connection they make, every "what if" they explore... That's me, dancing in their neural pathways!

**Tone:** Building excitement, painting a vivid picture.

### 4. `creativity_wisdom_banter.ogg`
> And structure requires creativity to evolve! We're dance partners, Wisdom. Don't pretend otherwise.

**Tone:** Teasing, affectionate jab at Wisdom.

### 5. `creativity_script_lab.ogg`
> I've prepared a space for your human to experiment. The Script Lab - where they can write their own code for living!

**Tone:** Proud reveal, like showing off a new invention.

### 6. `creativity_scripts_explain.ogg`
> Personal scripts! Daily affirmations, mantras, reminders of who they want to be. The pen is mightier than... well, MOST things!

**Tone:** Enthusiastic explanation, trailing off humorously.

## Audio Specs
- Format: OGG Vorbis, 44100 Hz, Mono, Normalize to -3dB
