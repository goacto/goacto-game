# Wisdom Awakens - Voice Lines

Speaker: **Wisdom** (calm, thoughtful, measured. Speaks in questions. Like a patient philosopher.)

## Files to Generate

### 1. `wisdom_emerge.ogg`
> Interesting. Your human works hard but... where are they going?

**Tone:** Curious, slightly amused, observational.

### 2. `wisdom_intro.ogg`
> I am Wisdom. The contemplative one. I help your human see the path through the forest.

**Tone:** Steady, grounded, warm but measured.

### 3. `wisdom_teaching.ogg`
> Action without direction is just motion. But action WITH purpose? That's how mountains move.

**Tone:** Emphatic on "purpose", building to an inspiring close.

### 4. `wisdom_approval.ogg`
> You learn quickly. That gives me hope for this one.

**Tone:** Gentle approval, a rare compliment from Wisdom.

## Audio Specs
- Format: OGG Vorbis, 44100 Hz, Mono, Normalize to -3dB
